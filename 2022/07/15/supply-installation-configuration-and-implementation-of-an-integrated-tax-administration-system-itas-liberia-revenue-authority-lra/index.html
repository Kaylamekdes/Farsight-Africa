Supply, Installation, Configuration and Implementation of an Integrated Tax Administration System (ITAS) – Liberia Revenue Authority (LRA)

Client
Liberia Revenue Authority (LRA)
Country
Liberia
Project implementation
February 2021 – Ongoing
Services
Technology & Innovation · Public Sector Advisory

Project summary

The Liberia Revenue Authority (LRA) needed a credible firm to supply, install, configure and implement an Integrated Tax Administration System. The Public Financial Management Reform for Institutional Strengthening launched this project, jointly funded by the World Bank, to enable LRA to achieve efficiently its objectives in implementing, leveraging and continuously improving a new ITAS solution based on best practices.

We have been tasked with the supply, installation, configuration, integration and support of all hardware equipment and overall project management.

Outcome

LRA is aiming to deploy a modern tax administration application that will help meet the following desired outcomes:

  • Improved taxpayer registration through online registration
  • Improved online and on-time filing
  • Improved online and on-time tax payment
  • Increase tax revenue
  • Increase taxpayer compliance levels

We are working with our implementation partners to ensure these are realised through the ITAS system.
