Gender Dimensions in Affected Communities Related to UNICEF Somalia Interventions – UNICEF

Client
UNICEF Somalia
Country
Somalia
Project implementation
2019
Services
M&E Research and Learning

Project summary

The study entails a survey of gender dimensions of various aspects of UNICEF programming in Somalia. The study includes household surveys, key informant interviews, focus group discussions (FGDs), and institutional capacity assessment of WASH, nutrition, child protection and other thematic areas.

The target locations were Somaliland, Puntland and central south regions.
