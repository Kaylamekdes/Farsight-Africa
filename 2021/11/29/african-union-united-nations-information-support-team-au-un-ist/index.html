Multidisciplinary Communications Support Services – African Union/United Nations Information Support Team

Client
African Union / United Nations, Somalia
Country
Somalia
Project implementation
2014 – 2019
Services
Strategic Communication · M&E Research and Learning

Project summary

Farsight was tasked with developing multi-disciplinary information support services in the areas of: research and monitoring through baseline, endline and midline surveys, FGDs and KIIs; advocacy and outreach campaigns; media monitoring and analytics; capacity building and production of novelty items. The engagement activities included:

  • Awareness programmes and training workshops in Mogadishu and the regions
  • Coordination and set up of stakeholders and partners for engagement, including regional and federal administrations, media, community leaders, religious leaders and opinion leaders
  • Media relations and liaison
  • Counter terrorism and radicalisation
  • Community policing and traditional dispute resolution

Outcome

We then leveraged our partner networks and contacts to provide additional support and advocacy of project initiatives, enhancing Somali diplomacy and media representation globally.

Farsight Africa’s professional expertise, local knowledge and strategic leverage delivered cohesive and effective results at all levels. This project did not just fulfil the client brief, but also enhanced the Farsight reputation with organisations and stakeholders in Somalia.
