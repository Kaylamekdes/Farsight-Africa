What we do

Four services, designed to work together

Our service array is comprehensive, so we can design our support to match the challenge and achieve the specific effects our clients need.

  1. 01

    Public Sector Advisory

    Advice to government. Ministries, agencies and international funders: public finance and revenue, security sector reform, HR advisory, and training and capacity building.

    Public Sector Advisory service page
  2. 02

    Technology & Innovation

    Systems for government. Tax revenue collection and administration, financial management and information systems, including the Integrated Tax Administration System for the Liberia Revenue Authority. In Somalia, our subsidiary Hornswitch offers a national payment gateway platform.

    Technology & Innovation service page
  3. 03

    M&E Research and Learning

    Evidence for decisions. Baseline, midline and endline surveys, focus groups and stakeholder consultations for evidence-based policy and strategic planning.

    M&E Research and Learning service page
  4. 04

    Strategic Communication

    Reaching the public. Outreach campaigns, media monitoring and analysis, social media, content production, live events and branding that explain programmes to the people they serve.

    Strategic Communication service page
