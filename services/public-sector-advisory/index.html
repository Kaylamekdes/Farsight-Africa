Introduction

Farsight Africa’s Public Sector Advisory service has a proven track record in all levels of government institutions, including ministries, agencies and international funding organisations, working in a transparent, accountable and efficient way.

Our experience in the public sector can also provide advisory services to commercial and non-profit organisations that are planning to work with governments or respond to tenders, where we can provide guidance and up to date status reports essential for successful engagement.

Public Finance

Farsight Africa’s Public Finance and Revenue management services provide enhanced automation in tax collection, local revenue mobilisation, collection and accountability. Our solutions assist revenue authorities, ministries of finance and local government authorities to enhance revenue administration and collection.

Security Sector Reforms

Farsight Africa Group has been engaged in security sector reform through security and conflict analysis and systematic research in the economic, political and social dynamics of security and conflict. Our team comprises certified security specialists with senior level experience in some of the world’s most acute scenarios, working with national military operations and some of the world’s largest private security organisations.

We recognise the importance of developing national and regional security and the contribution it makes to social development, humanitarian work and peace building. We can also advise organisations in conflict or post-conflict locations to gain a better understanding of the context in which they work and their regional role in the context of unstable environments.

HR Advisory Services

We provide a comprehensive range of HR services to public and private commercial organisations alike. With our in-depth understanding of the client’s operating environment, we meet workforce needs with specialists overlaid with Farsight’s years of expertise. We achieve this by:

  • Providing appropriate candidates, going the extra mile to transform your HR services
  • Providing end-to-end HR solutions to anticipate and meet business needs
  • Enhancing workforce experience through training and development solutions
  • Building the transformative capabilities of HR professionals so they can contribute to organisational resilience and continue to deliver regardless of the difficulties of the particular environment

Training and Capacity Building

Farsight Africa Group has provided training services to UNOPS, the African Development Bank and the US State Department in our capacity building programmes for the Federal Government of Somalia. Courses have included programmes for Leadership, Governance and Management at senior levels and operational training for senior officers, general staff and new recruits.

We have additionally delivered policy-building training and advice to significant commercial enterprises in East Africa.

Complementary Advisory Services

After consultation, our professional team will prepare the advisory services to meet your requirements, selecting from the range above and additional initiatives as needed, such as:

  • Financial Management
  • Business Development: Analysis, Training and Planning
  • Government and Local Authority Relations
  • Security and Conflict Analysis and Risk Mitigation
